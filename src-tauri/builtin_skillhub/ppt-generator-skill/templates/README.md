# PPT Templates

The built-in 9X bot package implements these template names inside
`scripts/generate.js`. Users may place custom `.pptx` files in this directory,
but the default generator does not require external template files.

| Name | Alias | Palette | Use Case |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 商务蓝 | business-blue | Midnight business blue | Business reports, finance |
| 科技感 | tech-dark | Dark technology blue | Technology products, internet |
| 清新绿 | fresh-green | Sage and mint | Education, training, health |
| 高端黑 | premium-black | Black and gold | Premium proposals, brand launches |
| 珊瑚活力 | coral-energy | Coral and gold | Creative marketing |
| 暖陶简约 | warm-terracotta | Terracotta and sage | Education, culture |
| MBE插画 | mbe-illustration | White, black outline, vivid accents | Campus, youth |
| 复古卡通 | retro-cartoon | Cream yellow, ink outline | Creative, hand-drawn |
